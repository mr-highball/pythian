[Task index](README.md) · [Task flow](../TASKFLOW.MD) · [North star](../MILESTONES.md#ns-5)

**Description:**

Learn recurring motifs, phrase/section boundaries, transitions and variation from recorded musical providers rather than only local grain compatibility.

North star: NS-5. Outcome owner: SONG-STRUCTURE.
Completion credit: 5 goal percentage points (1.00 overall points).
Credit is earned only when every acceptance criterion and the task-flow completion requirements pass.

Starting evidence: [LAYERED-STYLE](../LAYERED-STYLE.md) · [CORPUS-EVALUATION](../CORPUS-EVALUATION.md) · [PASSAGES](../PASSAGES.md).

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Declare supported phrase/section labels, boundary uncertainty and reference scope on verified recordings; processing chunks are not assumed to be song sections.
- Use admitted context, roles, harmony and groove to retain repeated motifs, variation, section relationships and transition evidence across independent songs.
- Pass frozen independent structural comparisons against declared baselines, with boundary and repetition/relationship coverage limits.
- Preserve source/run boundaries, gaps and uncertain segmentation through the maintained representation; unknown long-source song boundaries must not create cross-song learning.
- Expose reusable structural evidence suitable for semantic persistence and generation, with declared native computation/state bounds.
