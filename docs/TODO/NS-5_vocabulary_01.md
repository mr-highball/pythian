**Description:**

Prevent long or repetitive recordings from dominating learned styles while retaining supported musical evidence across sources.

North star: NS-5. Outcome owner: WAV-04-VOCABULARY.
Completion credit: 5 goal percentage points (1.00 overall points).
Credit is earned only when every acceptance criterion and the task-flow completion requirements pass.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Declare separate recording, unique-duration, excerpt and repeated-section contributions; keep training weights distinct from generation preferences and hard locks. The bounded admitted-pitch work-group token-mass comparison is covered by [NS-5_vocabulary_03](DONE/NS-5_vocabulary_03.md); the broader contribution accounting remains here.
- Implement representative vocabulary selection with explicit unknown/coverage/diversity and capacity criteria on verified multi-recording development material. This full vocabulary and corpus-coverage requirement remains here.
- Retain original source frames and absolute measurements under any relative-pitch or beat-relative normalization; normalization requires admitted references rather than guessed key/BPM. This remains here.
- Compare balanced and unbalanced learning under fixed input identities and budgets, showing the declared coverage/diversity criteria and source contribution accounting. [NS-5_vocabulary_03](DONE/NS-5_vocabulary_03.md) supplies only the bounded group-token-mass comparison; this task retains the complete vocabulary coverage/diversity comparison.
- Use maintained native APIs and actual WFC training with independent source/run boundaries; lower average feature distance alone cannot establish success. The bounded admitted-pitch WFC rebuild in [NS-5_vocabulary_03](DONE/NS-5_vocabulary_03.md) is prerequisite evidence, not completion of this broader criterion.
