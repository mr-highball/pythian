# NS-5_vocabulary_01 — Learn balanced, representative reusable vocabularies

[Task index](README.md) · [Task flow](../TASKFLOW.MD) · [North star](../MILESTONES.md#ns-5)

**Description:**

Prevent long or repetitive recordings from dominating learned styles while retaining supported musical evidence across sources.

North star: NS-5. Outcome owner: WAV-04-VOCABULARY.
Completion credit: 6 goal percentage points (1.20 overall points).
Credit is earned only when every acceptance criterion and the task-flow completion requirements pass.

Starting evidence: [CORPUS-EVALUATION](../CORPUS-EVALUATION.md) · [ANALYSIS-WAVE](../ANALYSIS-WAVE.md) · [LAYERED-STYLE](../LAYERED-STYLE.md).

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Declare separate recording, unique-duration, excerpt and repeated-section contributions; keep training weights distinct from generation preferences and hard locks.
- Implement representative vocabulary selection with explicit unknown/coverage/diversity and capacity criteria on verified multi-recording development material.
- Retain original source frames and absolute measurements under any relative-pitch or beat-relative normalization; normalization requires admitted references rather than guessed key/BPM.
- Compare balanced and unbalanced learning under fixed input identities and budgets, showing the declared coverage/diversity criteria and source contribution accounting.
- Use maintained native APIs and actual WFC training with independent source/run boundaries; lower average feature distance alone cannot establish success.
