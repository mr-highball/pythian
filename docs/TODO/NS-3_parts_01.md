**Description:**

Deliver the external development/evaluation reference packet that makes mixture
learning measurable: bound sources, reviewed musical annotations, qualified
recording families and explicit scenario coverage. Reuse the completed measurement
APIs and authored controls rather than extending a diagnostic tool chain.

North star: NS-3. Outcome owner: WAV-03-PARTS.
Completion credit: 1 goal percentage points (0.25 overall points).
Credit is earned only when every acceptance criterion and the task-flow completion requirements pass.

[reference builder](../PART-REFERENCE.md), [scenario inventory](../PART-EVALUATION.md#preparation-coverage-audit--2026-09-21)
and [frozen mixture policy](../PART-MIXTURE-POLICY.md).

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Bind usable external WAV stems and corresponding mixes with verified recording identities, original clocks, offsets and gains; retain all contributors, full notices and reviewed time-local role annotations.
- Provide useful external bass, chordal and lead/other reference evidence, complete known intervals/sets where supported and explicit uncertainty elsewhere. Account for simultaneous notes, crossings, quiet/masked parts, rests and uncertain ownership in a scenario ledger that distinguishes authored-only, external development, evaluation and missing coverage; never identify voices from stereo channels alone.
- Qualify and freeze at least one development family and a separate evaluation family through source-work identity and bounded duplicate/derivative review. Keep related stems, mixes and derivatives together, record reference-only exposure separately from tuning, and leave unresolved membership provisional.
- Apply the frozen supported-mixture, annotation and per-role threshold policy before evaluation predictions. Retain uncertain challenges and missing coverage without claiming complete-reference or independent accuracy; harmonic/percussive separation is not a role label.
- Assemble a reproducible native packet with source/preparation/annotation/scoring identities, case inventory, reconstruction and scoring instructions, replay/preservation evidence and accurate generic WAV provenance. Keep assets/manifests under ignored output and publish the maintained consumer instructions.
