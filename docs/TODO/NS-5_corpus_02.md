# NS-5_corpus_02 — Populate the chillwave training and evaluation corpus

[Task index](README.md) · [Task flow](../TASKFLOW.MD) · [North star](../MILESTONES.md#ns-5)

**Description:**

Supply representative, independently identified WAV material and frozen partitions for chillwave. Many hours and a genre label alone do not establish learning.

North star: NS-5. Outcome owner: CORPUS-SETUP.
Completion credit: 2 goal percentage points (0.40 overall points).
Credit is earned only when every acceptance criterion and the task-flow completion requirements pass.

Starting evidence: [CORPUS-EVALUATION](../CORPUS-EVALUATION.md).

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Meet the existing per-style planning floors: training >=3 unique hours from >=6 independent groups; development >=30 unique minutes from >=2 additional groups; evaluation >=30 unique minutes from >=3 additional unused groups.
- Ground the style assignment in documented recording traits and uncertainty; include relevant instrumentation, tempo/key, arrangement and section variety under the declared style card.
- Bind every source/section to verified identity, preparation/headroom, parent-range, duplicate and exposure audits; count unique coverage without overlapping excerpts or repeated passages inflating it.
- Freeze group-disjoint references and evaluation inputs before tuning/scoring; supply supported annotations or declared uncertainty for the required traits.
- Provide a reproducible native/operator input packet and truthful coverage report. Any justified change to a planning floor must precede evaluation and be recorded explicitly, not used to excuse a failed run.
