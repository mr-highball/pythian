# NS-6_adoption_01 — Establish independently supported ecosystem adoption

[Task index](README.md) · [Task flow](../TASKFLOW.MD) · [North star](../MILESTONES.md#ns-6)

**Description:**

Assess whether real maintained Pascal projects choose and continue to use
Pythian as a reusable synthesis/learning library. The standard-library ambition
requires observable ecosystem evidence beyond example reviewers or delivered
artifacts. Define the population and claim prospectively, obtain independent
use/preference evidence, and require a defensible maintainer verdict. External
adoption cannot be guaranteed by implementation tasks or project-count quotas.

North star: NS-6. Outcome owner: DELIVERY-ADOPTION.
Completion credit: 15 goal percentage points (1.50 overall points).
Current complete-goal basis: 2026-09-29; NS-6 weight 10, zero baseline, 15
accepted goal points and 85 open goal points. Its value is actual continued
ecosystem use and a justified adoption verdict, not activity or popularity
metrics. Creation of the task grants no acceptance.
Credit is earned only when every acceptance criterion and task-flow completion
requirement passes.

Starting evidence: [supported maintenance](NS-6_support_01.md) ·
[independent full delivery](NS-6_delivery_04.md) ·
[consumer contract](../CONSUMER-CONTRACT.md) · [north-star scope](../MILESTONES.md).

Execution status: externally blocked; no accepted ecosystem adoption verdict
is recorded, and support/full independent delivery remain prerequisites. Next deliverable: prospectively reviewed population/claim/threshold protocol followed
by independently attributable maintained-product use and update evidence.
Closing evidence: frozen protocol, downstream source/version/use identities,
maintainer feedback, continued use and the claim-limited independent assessment.
Stop when adoption is unavailable, the evidence cannot justify the claim or a
threshold would be changed merely to pass. Keep this task and full-goal
completion open; do not substitute agent demonstrations or promise future use.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- AC1: Before scoring, the maintainer reviews a prospective adoption protocol
  defining the intended Pascal ecosystem population, supported use scope,
  evidence sources/sampling, alternatives, independence, observation period and
  justified adoption/preference threshold. An initial quantitative floor is
  at least three unrelated maintained Pascal products across at least two
  independent maintainer groups, with actual library use beyond bundled
  examples and continued use across one supported Pythian update. The floor is
  necessary evidence, not a definition or proof of de facto standard status.
  Any revised threshold requires prospective evidence-based review, never a
  retroactive change to force acceptance.
- AC2: Obtain verifiable actual dependencies and synthesis/learning usage in
  those products under released, identifiable supported Pythian source/package
  versions. Record downstream revision/build/use identities, scope and ongoing
  maintenance without private details. Our own applications, agent/self-run
  checkouts, isolated example reproductions, proposed integrations, downloads,
  stars or expressions of interest do not satisfy product adoption.
- AC3: Record continued successful use across the supported update and actual
  independent maintainer feedback: why Pythian was selected, what alternatives
  were considered, practical benefits/limits and supported issues resolved or
  still open. Retain nonadoption/failed-use observations in the predeclared
  sampled population rather than selecting only favorable testimonials.
- AC4: Independently assess the evidence against the frozen protocol, including
  representativeness and comparative preference. Obtain the maintainer's
  explicit evidence-backed verdict on whether the standard-library/adoption
  claim is justified for the declared population. Meeting the numerical floor
  alone cannot close this criterion. If the evidence establishes only a few
  users or does not justify the intended claim, report that honestly, keep the
  criterion open and prevent full-goal 100% completion.
- AC5: Publish a reproducible scoped adoption assessment and supported handoff
  with exact use/evidence/version identities and limitations. Never widen a
  claim from the surveyed population to the whole ecosystem without supporting
  independent evidence. Broader de facto status remains unclaimed when that
  evidence is absent; tickets cannot guarantee market/ecosystem behavior.
  Publication and outreach still require their separate authorization.
