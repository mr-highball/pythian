# NS-5_blends_01 — Accept cross-style blends and further reuse

[Task index](README.md) · [Task flow](../TASKFLOW.MD) · [North star](../MILESTONES.md#ns-5)

**Description:**

Prove that accepted chillwave, stoner rock and lofi styles remain useful and controllable when selectively combined and blended again.

North star: NS-5. Outcome owner: STYLE-EVAL.
Completion credit: 6 goal percentage points (1.20 overall points).
Credit is earned only when every acceptance criterion and the task-flow completion requirements pass.

Starting evidence: [CORPUS-EVALUATION](../CORPUS-EVALUATION.md) · [WAVE-STYLE](../WAVE-STYLE.md).

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Evaluate all three pairwise style combinations and one further blend involving all three, using the declared seeds, duration and listening/trait protocol.
- Predeclare which layers/traits come from each parent and which combine by weight; retain independent context, rhythm, bass/voice and sound selection.
- Meet the declared inherited/combined trait and sustained-usefulness criteria with actual listening verdicts, explicit unknowns and reported regressions/conflicts.
- Verify source/ancestor overlap accounting, compatible normalization, save/reload replay and dependency-preserving paired edits through the final derived profile.
- Demonstrate the resulting derived style can seed further generation and another blend under the same supported contract without collapsing lineage or silently dropping constraints.
