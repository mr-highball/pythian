# NS-6_delivery_04 — Obtain independently reproduced consumer acceptance

[Task index](README.md) · [Task flow](../TASKFLOW.MD) · [North star](../MILESTONES.md#ns-6)

**Description:**

Have a consumer outside the implementation's own fixtures reproduce the supported library workflow and provide actionable feedback.

North star: NS-6. Outcome owner: DELIVERY-RELEASE.
Completion credit: 12 goal percentage points (0.60 overall points).
Credit is earned only when every acceptance criterion and the task-flow completion requirements pass.

Starting evidence: [PACKAGING](../PACKAGING.md) · [PROJECT](../../PROJECT.md).

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Provide the verified package, concise instructions and usable declared inputs to an independent reviewer/consumer under the agreed distribution policy.
- Obtain actual evidence that the reviewer builds the core/companion subset and completes the agreed load/control/generate/save/blend/further-blend workflow outside this checkout.
- Record the consumer's environment, artifact identities, success/failure observations and listening/usability feedback without exposing private details.
- Resolve blockers in supported behavior/documentation and recheck the affected package/consumer path; our own fixture runs cannot substitute for independent reproduction.
- Record a scoped acceptance verdict and support handoff. Track broader ecosystem adoption separately; do not promise de facto standard status from one successful consumer.
