# NS-3_groove_01 — Learn and accept rhythmic phrasing and groove

[Task index](README.md) · [Task flow](../TASKFLOW.MD) · [North star](../MILESTONES.md#ns-3)

**Description:**

Learn beat-relative rhythmic phrasing, accents and microtiming with their metrical and role relationships.

North star: NS-3. Outcome owner: WAV-02-GROOVE.
Completion credit: 6 goal percentage points (1.50 overall points).
Credit is earned only when every acceptance criterion and the task-flow completion requirements pass.

Starting evidence: [ONSET-DYNAMICS](../ONSET-DYNAMICS.md) · [BEAT-TRACKING](../BEAT-TRACKING.md) · [LAYERED-STYLE](../LAYERED-STYLE.md).

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Declare supported swing, syncopation, accents, timing deviation and articulation behaviors, with independent timing references and uncertainty limits.
- Use admitted clocks and roles to separate tempo movement from local groove; preserve original frames and explicit quantization/normalization policy.
- Retain joint rhythmic/velocity/role behavior without concatenating songs or filling observation gaps with invented onsets.
- Pass frozen independent timing/trait coverage and error criteria plus paired native reconstruction/listening; onset counts on a fixed authored grid are insufficient.
- Deliver maintained native provider evidence and practical cost for downstream WFC/style learning, retaining unsupported meter or ambiguous timing explicitly.
