# NS-3_harmony_01 — Learn and accept harmonic relationships

[Task index](README.md) · [Task flow](../TASKFLOW.MD) · [North star](../MILESTONES.md#ns-3)

**Description:**

Learn supported harmonic progressions and their relationship to bass and melody from admitted recorded evidence.

North star: NS-3. Outcome owner: WAV-02-HARMONY.
Completion credit: 6 goal percentage points (1.50 overall points).
Credit is earned only when every acceptance criterion and the task-flow completion requirements pass.

Starting evidence: [LAYERED-STYLE](../LAYERED-STYLE.md) · [JOINT](../JOINT.md) · [INDEPENDENT-VOICES](../INDEPENDENT-VOICES.md).

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Declare chord/harmonic vocabulary, change boundaries, relative versus absolute pitch references and unknown/ambiguous cases before evaluation.
- Use admitted context and role events to retain observed vertical intervals, bass/melody relationships and transitions; do not infer joint observations from unrelated marginals.
- Meet frozen reference-based chord/change and relationship coverage/error criteria on independent recordings after development controls pass.
- Keep source-specific absolute evidence and recoverable normalization, including missing/uncertain fundamentals and excluded spans.
- Expose reusable harmonic evidence/models through maintained native learning interfaces and demonstrate constrained reconstruction; authored accompaniment and chroma ranking alone do not pass.
