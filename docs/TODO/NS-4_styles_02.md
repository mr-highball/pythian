# NS-4_styles_02 — Support selective blend and further blend

[Task index](README.md) · [Task flow](../TASKFLOW.MD) · [North star](../MILESTONES.md#ns-4)

**Description:**

Make a derived semantic style a full input to later generation and selective blends, retaining joint relationships and auditable source contributions.

North star: NS-4. Outcome owner: WFC-STYLE.
Completion credit: 4 goal percentage points (0.60 overall points).
Credit is earned only when every acceptance criterion and the task-flow completion requirements pass.

Starting evidence: [WAVE-STYLE](../WAVE-STYLE.md) · [LAYERED-STYLE](../LAYERED-STYLE.md) · [CORPUS-EVALUATION](../CORPUS-EVALUATION.md).

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Combine supported rhythm, bass/voice, harmony and sound providers independently, with explicit selection, weighting, normalization and conflict handling.
- Resolve parent/ancestor overlap and repeated evidence through a recorded contribution policy; demonstrate no accidental double-counting or cross-song transitions.
- Save/reload a blend and a further blend, retaining full required lineage, independent preferences/locks and inherited/overridden provider identities.
- Demonstrate unrelated accepted layers remain unchanged under paired provider edits; reject or explicitly convert incompatible role, vocabulary, pitch and timing contracts.
- Use the actual learner/model composition contracts; do not average model bytes, merge unrelated acoustic indices or relabel generated material as verified recorded evidence.
