**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Register verified song/recording identities, source/derivative hashes, sample clocks, parent ranges, preparation policies and explicit or uncertain song boundaries.
- Audit duplicate/related recordings, stems, alternate masters and overlapping excerpts; keep each leakage family in one split and retain prior exposure after re-encoding.
- Resolve preparation/headroom exclusions for the full-scale sections and retain their family/exposure history; document generic WAV sources while keeping local asset details ignored.
- Populate a real multi-recording development pilot with at least two verified independent recording groups and bind the existing operator declarations to the complete identity/overlap audit.
- Demonstrate split/exposure and outside-plan/frozen-palette ancestry checks across reuse, not merely exact-hash equality. Existing unverified A/B/C material remains development-only until identity is established and can never become untouched by relabeling.
