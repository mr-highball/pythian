**Description:**

Deliver a reusable, source-bound stream over multiple Pascal-produced WAV
observation artifacts and qualify it on the prepared 2.322-hour development

admission; raw salience is not an admitted note or a learned style.

closed no criterion. The semantic-learning and whole-pipeline gates stay there.

credit. No new source or genre identity is asserted for the three inputs.

from the four unearned points of `NS-5_scale_01`. The two tasks retain the
original four-point allocation. Credit is earned only after every criterion
and task-flow completion requirement passes.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Freeze the exact three prepared development WAV identities, parent ranges,
  conservative recording groups, five adjacent processing scopes, selected
  channel, estimator/policy and work/storage limits before running. Count
  2.3222053373 original-clock hours once; the long mix is one group with
  unresolved internal song boundaries, and this is not genre corpus coverage.
- Provide a maintained Pascal reader/consumer that validates every artifact's
  source, geometry, estimator, policy, complete checksum and center sequence;
  stream bounded batches with explicit source/group and scope identity. Reject
  overlap, missing scope, wrong binding and corruption without flattening
  recording boundaries or loading the corpus into one observation array.
- Run the existing supervised Pascal producer on all five scopes within the
  frozen 8,510-second combined raw-job ceiling, existing per-job scope-plus-30-
  second and 2-GiB worker-private bounds, 2-GiB raw-cache budget and 8-GiB
  working-storage bound. Preserve ten full-source verification passes and
  report the actual per-job/total costs; no external inference runtime.
- Reconcile exact source-clock coverage, expected observation counts and all
  emitted artifact identities through the maintained reader. Pass focused
  checked-target boundary/replay/failure tests and one actual Win64 corpus
  reading; preserve prior accepted artifacts on failed publication.
- Publish a reproducible native input/command and result packet that later
  admitted-note learning can consume without inventing semantic run boundaries.
  Report raw coverage only; no note, role, style or full-pipeline acceptance.
