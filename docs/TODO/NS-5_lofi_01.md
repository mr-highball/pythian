# NS-5_lofi_01 — Accept reusable lofi learning

[Task index](README.md) · [Task flow](../TASKFLOW.MD) · [North star](../MILESTONES.md#ns-5)

**Description:**

Train and independently accept a useful lofi style from its representative many-recording WAV corpus. This closes the style-specific result, not just the training machinery.

North star: NS-5. Outcome owner: STYLE-EVAL.
Completion credit: 6 goal percentage points (1.20 overall points).
Credit is earned only when every acceptance criterion and the task-flow completion requirements pass.

Starting evidence: [CORPUS-EVALUATION](../CORPUS-EVALUATION.md) · [LAYERED-STYLE](../LAYERED-STYLE.md).

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Freeze the final provider/training/weighting/generation policy before using this style's untouched evaluation groups; verify the populated coverage packet and all required provider support.
- Run the complete fixed-seed comparison packet and pass the predeclared quantitative trait, unknown/coverage, source-use, continuity and structural criteria.
- Record actual listening scores for every required trait, continuity/structure and usefulness; each required dimension must reach at least 2 on the declared 0..3 scale with no invalidating synthesis defect.
- Demonstrate a repeatable predeclared coverage or musical-relationship advantage over matched baselines; report regressions and do not substitute recognizable source fragments or input hours for learning.
- Save/reload the accepted style and perform within-style blend/further blend and paired granular edits, retaining expected traits, source contributions and dependency preservation.
- Publish a bounded acceptance verdict with complete artifact/evidence identities. Tuning after evaluation requires fresh independent material; failed criteria keep this task open.
