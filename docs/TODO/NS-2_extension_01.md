**Acceptance Criteria:**

- AC1: Deliver a documented external consumer with its own Pascal source factory and audio effect, compiled against the extracted current package only. Supply at least one parameterized sound behavior and one stateful processor; no Pythian or dependency source edits, private include paths or hidden assets are allowed.
- AC2: Exercise the public ownership, clone/reset, range, work-estimate and failure contracts through scheduled and streamed synthesis. Verify the caller's conservative work estimates and reject declared over-budget work before execution; accurate cost declarations remain caller obligations, not runtime enforcement against arbitrary caller code. Invalid parameters or nonfinite output cannot silently contaminate accepted state.
- AC3: Verify fixed-seed deterministic replay across varied block/read sizes, complete release/reset, event-boundary behavior, and fault/retry preservation on the supported native targets. Describe the precise caller obligations and failure limits; no hard real-time or arbitrary plug-in safety claim.
- AC4: Record actual listening to the extended output at its declared settings, with no invalidating synthesis defect, plus exact source/package/parameter identities. Built-in examples cannot substitute for the caller-owned units.
- AC5: Publish a reusable extension guide and conformance example that another caller can adapt without private assistance. External-use evidence, supported interfaces and limitations are explicit.
