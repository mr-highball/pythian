Record from your phone

Open the HTTPS page, then allow microphone access.

  1. Open HTTPS Studio in Brave
  2. Tap Record audio, then Start microphone and allow microphone permission.
Optional: remove the certificate warning

If your browser allows recording after you accept this local computer’s certificate warning, no installation is needed. Otherwise:

  1. Download this computer’s certificate
  2. In Android Settings, install it as a CA certificate, then reopen HTTPS Studio.

Match the certificate’s SHA-256 fingerprint to this computer:

@@PYTHIAN_CERT_SHA256@@

To remove this trust later, remove this certificate from Android Settings under user-installed credentials or trusted certificates.
